The #else directive
If the condition specified in the #if, #ifdef,
or #ifndef directive evaluates to 0, and the
conditional compilation directive contains a preprocessor #else directive,
the lines of code located between the preprocessor #else directive
and the preprocessor #endif directive is selected
by the preprocessor to be passed on to the compiler.
