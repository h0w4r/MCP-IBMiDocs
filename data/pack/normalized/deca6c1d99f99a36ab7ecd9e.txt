END-UNSTRING Phrase
This explicit scope terminator serves to delimit the scope of the UNSTRING
statement. END-UNSTRING permits a conditional UNSTRING statement to be nested
in another conditional statement. END-UNSTRING may also be used with an imperative
UNSTRING statement.
For more information, see Delimited Scope Statements.
