PROCPTR

The PROCPTR keyword defines an item as a procedure pointer. The internal
Data-Type field (position 40) must contain a *.

See EXTPROC{({*CL|*CWIDEN|*CNOWIDEN|*JAVA:class-name:}name|*DCLCASE)} for information on how to use a procedure pointer
to call a procedure.

Note: The PROCPTR keyword is not used in
a free-form definition.
Instead, keyword POINTER(*PROC)
is specified to define the item as a procedure pointer.
