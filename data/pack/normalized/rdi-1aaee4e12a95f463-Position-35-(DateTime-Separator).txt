Position 35 (Date/Time Separator)

Position 35 specifies a separator character to be used for date/time fields.
The & (ampersand) can be used to specify a blank separator. See Table 1 and Table 1 for date and time formats and
their default separators.

For an entry to be made in this field, an entry must also be made in positions
31-34 (date/time external format).
