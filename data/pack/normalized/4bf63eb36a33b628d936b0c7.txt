LINKAGE TYPE Clause Considerations
There are several ways to affect the type of linkage generated for a CALL,
CANCEL, or SET. They are listed in order of precedence. The LINKAGE phrase
of the CALL, CANCEL, or SET statement has the highest precedence. If no LINKAGE
phrase is specified on the statement and there is no visible nested program,
the LINKAGE TYPE clause is used if specified. The order of precedence is:
The LINKAGE phrase of the statement
CALL or CANCEL to a nested program
The LINKAGE TYPE clause of the SPECIAL-NAMES paragraph
The LINKLIT parameter of the CRTCBLMOD or CRTBNDCBL command
