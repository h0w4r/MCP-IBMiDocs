Representation of Numeric Formats

Figure 1 shows what the decimal number 21544 looks like in
various formats.

Figure 1. Representation of the Number
21544 in each of the Numeric Formats

Note the following about the representations in the figure.

To obtain the numeric value of a positive binary or integer number, unsigned
number, add the values of the bits that are on (1), but do not include the
sign bit (if present). For an unsigned number, add the values of the bits
that are on, including the leftmost bit.

The value 21544 cannot be represented in a 2-byte binary field even though
it only uses bits in the low-order two bytes. A 2-byte binary field can only
hold up to 4 digits, and 21544 has 5 digits.
