Edit Words

An edit word specifies the punctuation of numeric fields, including
the printing of dollar signs, commas, periods, and sign status. See Parts of an Edit Word for details.

Edit words must be character literals or named constants. Graphic,
UCS-2, or hexadecimal literals and named constants are not allowed.
