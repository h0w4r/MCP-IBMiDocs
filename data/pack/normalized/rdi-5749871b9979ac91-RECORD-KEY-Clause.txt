RECORD KEY Clause
The RECORD KEY clause must be specified for an indexed file. The
RECORD KEY clause specifies the data item within the record that is
the record key for an indexed file.

RECORD KEY Clause - Format

    (1)    
>>-RECORD--+-----+--+----+--+-EXTERNALLY-DESCRIBED-KEY-----+---->
    '-KEY-' '-IS-' '-data-name-2------------------'   

>--+--------------------------+--------------------------------><
   | (1) |   
   '-+------+--DUPLICATES-----'   
    '-WITH-'    

Notes:IBM® Extension
