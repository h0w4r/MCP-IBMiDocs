INZTAP, DUPTAP, and RMVTAPCTG command authority changes

Last Updated: 2025-11-20

Save System (*SAVSYS) special authority is now required to use the Initialize Tape
(INZTAP), Duplicate Tape (DUPTAP), and the Remove Tape
Cartridge (RMVTAPCTG) commands.
