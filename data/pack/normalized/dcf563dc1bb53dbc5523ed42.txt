AT Phrase
The AT phrase indicates the starting position in the data area from which
text is received.
If the AT phrase is not specified, a starting position of 1 is assumed.
identifier-4, integer-1
Identifier-4 and integer-1 must be positive numeric integers with a
value that ranges from 1 to the maximum data area size (2000).
