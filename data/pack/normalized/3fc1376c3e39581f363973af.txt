Error messages

The Work with Automatically Scheduled Jobs display shows you when the PM Agent jobs are scheduled to run. You see the job name, status (A=active or I=inactive), next run date and time, and the number of runs to date.

Type 2 next to the job that you want to change and press Enter. You are shown the Change Automatically Scheduled Jobs display.

Press F5 (Refresh) to refresh the display with current job information.
