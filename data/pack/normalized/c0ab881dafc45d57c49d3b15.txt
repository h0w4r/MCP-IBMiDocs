Raising Signals
Signals are raised implicitly or explicitly. To explicitly
raise a signal, use the raise() function. Signals are
implicitly raised by the iSeries system when an exception
occurs. For example, if you call a program that does not exist, an
implicit signal is raised indicating that the program object could not be
found.
