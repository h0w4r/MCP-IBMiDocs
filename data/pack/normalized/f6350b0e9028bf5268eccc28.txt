The Print System Information (PRTSYSINF) command prints system information that should be maintained for disaster recovery and system verification purposes. A record of the contents of your system, such as how your system is customized and what libraries it contains, is important to your upgrade success because the information helps you do the following:

- Plan your upgrade procedures

- Evaluate the success of moving information

- Perform disaster recovery, if necessary
