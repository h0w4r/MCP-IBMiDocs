ZERO-FILL Phrase
Non-edited data items appear on the screen with no zero-suppression. For
left-justified data, trailing spaces and trailing hexadecimal zeros are converted
to zeros. For right-justified data, leading spaces are converted to zeros.
This takes effect when initial data in the data item is displayed and again
when the ACCEPT operation into the data item is terminated. It has no effect
on edited fields.
