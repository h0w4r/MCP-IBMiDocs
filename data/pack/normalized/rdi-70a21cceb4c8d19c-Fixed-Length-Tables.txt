Fixed-Length Tables
Fixed-length tables are specified using the OCCURS clause. Because seven
subscripts or indexes are allowed, six nested levels and one outermost level
of the Format 1 OCCURS clause are allowed. The Format 1 OCCURS clause may
be specified as subordinate to the OCCURS DEPENDING ON clause. In this way,
a table of up to seven dimensions may be specified.

OCCURS Clause - Format 1 - Fixed-Length Tables

>>-OCCURS--integer-2--+-------+--------------------------------->
    '-TIMES-'   

   .------------------------------------------------------.   
   V |   
>----+--------------------------------------------------+-+----->
    | .-------------. |    
    | V | |    
    '-+-ASCENDING--+--+-----+--+----+----data-name-2-+-'    
    '-DESCENDING-' '-KEY-' '-IS-'    

>--+-----------------------------------+-----------------------><
   | .--------------. |   
   | V | |   
   '-INDEXED--+----+----index-name-1-+-'   
    '-BY-'    

integer-2
Specifies the exact number of occurrences, and must be greater than
zero.
In the ILE COBOL language, integer-2 must be between 1 and 16 711 568
bytes.
