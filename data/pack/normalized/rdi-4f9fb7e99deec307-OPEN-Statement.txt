OPEN Statement
The OPEN statement initiates the processing of files and
checks or writes labels.
The OPEN statement varies depending on the type of file.
The OPEN statement has three formats: Format 1 - Sequential Files
Format 2 - Indexed and Relative
Files
Format 3 - TRANSACTION Files
