AUTO (AUTO-SKIP) Phrase
When a field has been filled by operator input, the cursor automatically
steps to the next input field, rather than waiting for a terminating character
to be entered. If the field is the last in a group, AUTO-SKIP acts as if the
ENTER key had been pressed.
AUTO and AUTO-SKIP may be used interchangeably.
