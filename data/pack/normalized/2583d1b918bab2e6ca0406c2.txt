STRJRNPF (Start Journal Physical File) requires *READ authority to the file

Last Updated: 2025-10-07

To increase security and to align with ENDJRNPF (End Journal Physical File), the STRJRNPF (Start
Journal Physical File) command now requires *READ authority to the file in addition to the prior
requirements, *OBJMGT and *OBJOPR.
