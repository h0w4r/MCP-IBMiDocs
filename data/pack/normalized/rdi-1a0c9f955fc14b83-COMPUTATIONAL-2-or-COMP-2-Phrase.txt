COMPUTATIONAL-2 or COMP-2 Phrase
The COMPUTATIONAL-2 or COMP-2 phrase is specified for internal floating-point
items (double precision). COMP-2 items are 8 bytes long. The sign is contained
in the first bit of the leftmost byte and the next 11 bits contain the exponent.
The remaining 52 bits contain the mantissa. For conditional expressions, the
class condition cannot be used for COMPUTATIONAL-2 or COMP-2 internal floating-point
data items.
