The MHLZO operation moves the zone portion of a character from
the leftmost zone in factor 2 to the rightmost zone in the result
field. Factor 2 must be defined as a character field. The result
field can be character or numeric data. For further information on
the MHLZO operation, see Move Zone Operations.
