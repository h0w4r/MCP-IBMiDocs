Standalone Fields

Standalone fields allow you to define individual work fields. A
standalone field has the following characteristics:

It has a specifiable internal data type

It may be defined as an array, table, or field

It is defined in terms of data length, not in terms of absolute
byte positions.

When the BASED keyword
is not specified, standalone field is always initialized,
either to its default value or the value specified by the INZ keyword.
In the following example, fileName is initialized to
its default value of blanks, and libraryName is initialized to
"*LIBL", the value specified by the INZ keyword.

DCL-S fileName CHAR(10);
DCL-S libraryName CHAR(10) INZ('*LIBL');

You can specify the CONST
keyword to prevent the field from being changed.
In the following example, the startTime
field is initialized to the timestamp at the start of the program,
and it cannot be changed after initialization.

DCL-S startTime TIMESTAMP INZ(*SYS) CONST;
