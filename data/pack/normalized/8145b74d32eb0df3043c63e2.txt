INDICATORS Phrase
Specifies which indicators are to be used when a data record is
written. Indicators can be used to pass information about the data
record and how it was entered into the program.
For detailed information on the INDICATORS phrase, refer to Using
Indicators with Transaction Files in the IBM® Rational® Development Studio for i: ILE COBOL
Programmer's Guide.
Identifier-8 must be either an elementary Boolean data item specified
without the OCCURS clause or a group item that has elementary Boolean
data items subordinate to it.
Figure 1 shows an example of rolling.
An initial screen format, FMT1 is written on the work station screen.
The program processes this screen format and is now ready to write
the next screen format, FMT2, to the workstation screen. Part of FMT1
is rolled down 2 lines before FMT2 is written to the workstation screen.
Execution of the following WRITE statement causes part of FMT1
to be rolled down 2 lines, and FMT2 to be written to the workstation
screen:    WRITE SCREENREC FORMAT "FMT2"
    AFTER ROLLING LINES 14 THROUGH 20
    DOWN 2 LINES

When this WRITE statement is executed, the following steps occur:
The contents of lines 14 through 20 are rolled down 2 lines. The contents of lines 14 through 18 now appear on lines 16 through
20.
The contents of lines 14 and 15 are vacated and cleared.
The contents of lines 19 and 20 are rolled outside the window
and disappear.

After the rolling operation takes place, FMT2 is written to the
workstation screen. Part of FMT2 is written to the area vacated by the roll operation.
Part of FMT2 is written over the data left from FMT1.

When the contents of the workstation screen are returned to the
program by a READ statement, only the input capable fields of FMT2
are returned.
