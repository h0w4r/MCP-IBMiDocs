FULL (LENGTH-CHECK) Phrase
The operator must either leave the screen item completely empty or fill
it entirely with data. The FIELD-EXIT, FIELD+, FIELD- keys are not allowed.
Any attempt to use the delete key on the data within the input field, followed
by the enter key, is also not allowed. The FULL phrase can be satisfied by
data that is initially displayed.
If this phrase is specified at a group level, it applies to all suitable
subordinate elementary items.
The FULL phrase is effective during the execution of any ACCEPT statement.
FULL and LENGTH-CHECK may be used interchangeably.
