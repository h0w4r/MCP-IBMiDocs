Bitwise negation operator ~
The ~ (bitwise negation) operator yields the
bitwise complement of the operand. In the binary representation of
the result, every bit has the opposite value of the same bit in the
binary representation of the operand. The operand must have an integral
type. The result has the same type as the operand but is not an lvalue.
Suppose x represents the decimal value 5.
The 16-bit binary representation of x is:
0000000000000101
The expression ~x yields the following result
(represented here as a 16-bit binary number):
1111111111111010
Note that the ~ character can be represented
by the trigraph ??-.
The 16-bit binary representation of ~0 is:
1111111111111111
