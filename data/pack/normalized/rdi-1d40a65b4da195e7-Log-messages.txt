Log messages
There are two types of logs for messages: job logs and history
logs.
A job log contains information related to requests entered for a job. The
system history log (QHST) contains system data, such as a history of job start
and end activity on your system.

Job log
Each job has an associated job log.
QHST history log
The history log (QHST) consists of a message queue and a physical
file known as a log version.
