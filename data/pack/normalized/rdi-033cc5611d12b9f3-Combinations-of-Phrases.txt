Combinations of Phrases
When one ACCEPT or DISPLAY statement contains the UNDERLINE, HIGHLIGHT
and REVERSE-VIDEO phrases in one WITH phrase, the HIGHLIGHT phrase is ignored.
A warning message (LNC0265) is generated at compilation time if this combination
is coded. In an extended DISPLAY statement, the UPON CRT-UNDER phrase is equivalent
to the UNDERLINE phrase. To protect a field from being displayed on the screen,
use the SECURE option.
