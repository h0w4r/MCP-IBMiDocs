The SETOFF operation sets off any indicators specified in positions
71 through 76. You must specify at least one resulting indicator in
positions 71 through 76. Entries of 1P and MR are not valid. Setting
off L1 through L9 indicators does not automatically set off any lower
control-level indicators.

Figure 1 illustrates the SETOFF operation.
