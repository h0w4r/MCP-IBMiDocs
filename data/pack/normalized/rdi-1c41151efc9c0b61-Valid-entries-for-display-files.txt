Note: The data types J (only), E (either), O (open), and G (graphic)
support DDS display files that use DBCS. The G (graphic) data type also supports
DDS display files that use UTF-16 and UCS-2.

in Defining a display file for DDS and Date (L), Time (T), and Timestamp (Z) show
