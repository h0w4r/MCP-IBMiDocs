Formatting the Expansion of an Edit Word

The characters in the expansion portion of an edit word are always
used. The expansion cannot contain blanks. If a blank is required
in the edited result, specify an ampersand in the body of the edit
word.

Constants may be added to appear with any value of the number:

Source Data

Appears in Edited Result as:

'␢,␢␢0.␢␢&CR&NET'

␢␢␢␢1.23␢CR␢NET

'␢,␢␢0.␢␢&CR&NET'

000123

␢␢␢␢1.23␢␢␢␢NET

Note that the CR in the middle of a word may be detected as a negative
field value indication. If a word such as SECRET is required, use
the coding in the example below.
