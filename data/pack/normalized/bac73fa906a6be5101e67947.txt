Length of Parameters
If the length of any parameter (in bytes), as defined in the calling
program, does not match the length expected by the called program,
unexpected results could occur in the called or calling program. See
the section on "Passing and Sharing Data between Programs" in
the IBM® Rational® Development Studio
for i: ILE COBOL Programmer's Guidefor details.
