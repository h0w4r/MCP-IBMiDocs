STRIP
The STRIP function removes blanks or another specified character
from the end, the beginning, or both ends of a string expression.
STRIP(expression,BOTH,B,LEADING,L,TRAILING,T,strip-character)
The STRIP function is identical to the TRIM scalar function. For
more information, see TRIM.
