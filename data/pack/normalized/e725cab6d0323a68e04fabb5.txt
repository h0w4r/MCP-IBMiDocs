DataLink comparisons
A DATALINK operand cannot be directly compared to any data
type. The DLCOMMENT, DLLINKTYPE, DLURLCOMPLETE, DLURLPATH, DLURLPATHONLY,
DLURLSCHEME, and DLURLSERVER scalar functions can be used to extract
character string values from a datalink which can then be compared
to other strings.
