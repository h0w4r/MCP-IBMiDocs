COS
The COS function returns a numeric value that approximates the cosine of
the angle or arc specified by the argument in radians.
The function type is numeric.

Format

>>-FUNCTION COS--(--argument-1--)------------------------------><

argument-1
Must be class numeric.

The returned value is the approximation of the cosine of the argument,
and is greater than or equal to -1 and less than or equal to +1.
