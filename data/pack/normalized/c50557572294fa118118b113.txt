Comparing National Operands
An operand of class NATIONAL may be compared to another operand of class
NATIONAL. The result of such a comparison is based on the binary collating
sequence of the hexadecimal values of the UCS-2 character set. If two operands
have unequal size, the shorter one is padded to the right with the padding
character specified in the Padding Character compile option or the equvilant
process option. The default is the UCS-2 double-byte space character (NX"3000").
