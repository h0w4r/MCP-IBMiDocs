Keyword considerations for display files that use Unicode data (positions
45 through 80)

The DFT keyword can contain SBCS, bracketed-DBCS, or bracketed-DBCS-graphic
character strings when specified on a Unicode-capable field.

No validity checking is allowed for a field capable of Unicode.

The CCSID keyword specifies that a G-type field supports Unicode data instead
of DBCS-graphical data.
