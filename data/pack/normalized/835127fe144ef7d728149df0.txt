Delimited Scope Statements
A delimited scope statement uses an explicit scope terminator to turn a conditional statement into an imperative statement;
the resulting imperative statement can then be nested. Explicit scope terminators
may also be used to terminate the scope of an imperative statement. Explicit
scope terminators are provided for all COBOL verbs that may have conditional
phrases.
Unless explicitly specified otherwise, a delimited scope statement may
be specified wherever an imperative statement is allowed by the rules of the
language.
