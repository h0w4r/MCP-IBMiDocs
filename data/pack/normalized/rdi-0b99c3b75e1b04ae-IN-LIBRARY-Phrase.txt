IN LIBRARY Phrase
This phrase is only valid for canceling an IBM® i program
object. That is, a linkage of type program must be specified, either implicitly
or explicitly, on the CANCEL statement.
identifier-2
Must be an alphanumeric data item. The contents of identifier-2 must
represent a valid IBM i library name. IBM i library
names are at most 10 characters long. The first 10 characters of identifier-2
are used to form the library name.
literal-2
Must be nonnumeric and can be a maximum of 10 characters long.

Identifier-2 and literal-2 are not affected by
the *MONOPRC compiler option, and can contain an IBM i extended
name.
