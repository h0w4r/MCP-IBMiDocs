Open Feedback Area
The open feedback area is part of the open data path that contains
information about the open file that is associated with that open data
path. You can assign a pointer to this information by using the
_Ropnfbk() function. The structure that maps to the open
feedback area can be that is found in the <xxfdbk.h>
header file.
