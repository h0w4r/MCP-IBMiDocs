RECORD clause - Format 3
Format 3 specifies the number of character positions for either fixed or
variable length records. (The latter is only applicable to tape files.)

RECORD Clause - Format 3

>>-RECORD--+----------+--integer-6--TO--integer-7--+------------+-><
    '-CONTAINS-' '-CHARACTERS-'
