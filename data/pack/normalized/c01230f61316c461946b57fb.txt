Explicit Scope Terminators
An explicit scope terminator marks the end of certain Procedure Division
statements. A conditional statement that is delimited by its explicit scope
terminator is considered an imperative statement and must follow the rules
for imperative statements.
The following are explicit scope terminators:
END-ACCEPT END-PERFORM
END-ADD END-READ
END-CALL END-RETURN
END-COMPUTE END-REWRITE
END-DELETE END-SEARCH
END-DISPLAY END-START
END-DIVIDE END-STRING
END-EVALUATE END-SUBTRACT
END-IF END-UNSTRING
END-MULTIPLY END-WRITE
