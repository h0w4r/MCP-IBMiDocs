In-line PERFORM Statement
An in-line PERFORM statement functions according to the same general
rules as an otherwise identical out-of-line PERFORM statement, except that
statements contained within the in-line PERFORM are executed in place of the
statements contained within the range of procedure-name-1 (through procedure-name-2,
if specified). Unless specifically qualified by the word in-line or out-of-line, all the rules that apply
to the out-of-line PERFORM statement also apply to the in-line PERFORM.
