Datetime variables
Character string variables are normally used to contain
date, time, and timestamp values.
The ILE RPG and ILE COBOL precompilers support datetime
variables.
Date, time, and timestamp variables can also be specified
in Java™ as java.sql.Date, java.sql.Time,
and java.sql.Timestamp, respectively.
