Floating-point constants
A floating-point constant specifies a double-precision floating-point
number as two numbers separated by an E. The first number can include a sign
and a decimal point; the second number can include a sign but not a decimal
point. The value of the constant is the product of the first number and the
power of 10 specified by the second number; it must be within the range of
floating-point numbers. The number of characters in the constant must not
exceed 24. Excluding leading zeros, the number of digits in the first number
must not exceed 17 and the number of digits in the second must not exceed
3.
Examples15E1 2.E5 2.2E-1 +5.E+2
