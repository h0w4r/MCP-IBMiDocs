COMPUTATIONAL or COMP Phrase
The COMPUTATIONAL or COMP phrase is specified for internal decimal items.
Such an item appears in storage as 2 digits per byte, with the sign contained
in the 4 rightmost bits of the rightmost byte. An internal decimal item can
contain any of the digits 0 through 9 plus a sign. If the PICTURE of an internal
decimal item does not contain an S, the sign position is occupied by a bit
configuration that is interpreted as positive. Of all USAGEs, USAGE COMP is
the most efficient in terms of operational performance.
For the ILE COBOL compiler, the COMPUTATIONAL phrase is synonymous
with
USAGE COMP-4(Binary), if option COMPASBIN is specified
Otherwise, PACKED-DECIMAL.
