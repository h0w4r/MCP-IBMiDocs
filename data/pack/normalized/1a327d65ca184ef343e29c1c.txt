POINTER Phrase
identifier-7
Identifier-7, an integer data-item defined without the symbol P in the
PICTURE string, contains a value that indicates a relative position in the
sending field. When this phrase is specified, the user must initialize this
field before execution of the UNSTRING statement is begun.
