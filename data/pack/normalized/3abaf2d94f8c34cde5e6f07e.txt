Initialization

To initialize the Time field to the system time at runtime, specify INZ(*SYS)
on the definition specification. *SYS cannot be used with a field that is
exported. The Time field can also be initialized at runtime to a literal,
named constant or figurative constant.
Note: Runtime initialization
takes place after static intitialization.
