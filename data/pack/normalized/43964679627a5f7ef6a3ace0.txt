Conventions used in this book

Last Updated: 2025-10-07

This section specifies some conventions which are used throughout
this manual.

Highlighting conventions

Bold | Indicates SQL keywords used in examples and when introducing descriptions involving the
keyword.
Italics | Indicates one of the following:

- Variables that represent items from a syntax diagram.

- The introduction of a new term.

- A reference to another source of information.
