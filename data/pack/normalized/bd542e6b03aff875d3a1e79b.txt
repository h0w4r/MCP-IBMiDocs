Implicit Closing of Files and Unlocking of Data Areas

Global files that are open are closed implicitly, and data areas
that are locked are unlocked implicitly during cycle-main procedure
termination, when the cycle-main procedure ends abnormally or with
LR on. Automatic files in subprocedures are closed implicitly when
the subprocedure ends normally or abnormally.

Caution: There is no implicit closing of
static files in subprocedures. There is no closing of global files
or implicit unlocking of data areas in a linear module. These objects
will remain open or locked unless they are explicitly closed or unlocked.
