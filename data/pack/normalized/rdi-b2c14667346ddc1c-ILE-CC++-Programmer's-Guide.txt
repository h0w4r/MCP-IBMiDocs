Creation of Indicators in the File Buffer
If you do not specify the keyword INDARA in DDS, the indicators
are created as part of the file buffer being read or written. Only
those indicators that are used are declared in the type definition of the
structure. They are declared as char, and created when the
INPUT, OUTPUT, or BOTH option is specified on the #pragma mapinc
directive.
