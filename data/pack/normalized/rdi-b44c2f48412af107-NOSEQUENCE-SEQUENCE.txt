NOSEQUENCE / SEQUENCE
NOSEQUENCE indicates that the reference
numbers are not checked for sequence errors. NOSEQUENCE is the default value.
SEQUENCE indicates that the reference numbers are
checked for sequence errors. Sequence errors do not occur if the LINENUMBER
option is specified.
