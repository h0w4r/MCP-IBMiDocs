FLTDIV{(*NO | *YES)}

The FLTDIV keyword indicates that all divide operations within expressions
are computed in floating point and return a value of type float. If not specified
or specified with *NO, then divide operations are performed in packed-decimal
format (unless one of the two operands is already in float format).

If FLTDIV is specified, optionally with *YES, then all divide operations
are performed in float format (guaranteeing that the result always has 15
digits of precision).
