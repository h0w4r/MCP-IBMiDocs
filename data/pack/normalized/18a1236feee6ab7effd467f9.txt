SPACE-FILL Phrase
For non-edited data items, trailing hexadecimal zeros are converted to
spaces, and the items appear on the screen with zero-suppression in all character
positions. This takes effect when initial data in the data item is displayed
and again when the ACCEPT operation into the data item is terminated. This
option has no effect on edited fields.
