Packed-Decimal Format

Packed-decimal format means that each byte of storage (except for the low
order byte) can contain two decimal numbers. The low-order byte contains one
digit in the leftmost portion and the sign (positive or negative) in the rightmost
portion. The standard signs are used: hexadecimal F for positive numbers and
hexadecimal D for negative numbers. The packed-decimal format looks like this:

The sign portion of the low-order byte indicates whether the numeric value
