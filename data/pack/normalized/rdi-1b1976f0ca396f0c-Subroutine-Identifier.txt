Subroutine Identifier

An SR entry in positions 7 and 8 may optionally be used for operations
within subroutines as a documentation aid. Subroutine lines must appear after
the total calculation specifications. The operation codes BEGSR and ENDSR
serve as delimiters for a subroutine.
