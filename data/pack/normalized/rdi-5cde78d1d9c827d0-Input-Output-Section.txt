Input-Output Section
The Input-Output Section defines each file, identifies its external storage
medium, assigns the file to one or more input/output devices, and specifies
information needed for transmission of data between the external medium and
the COBOL program.
The Input-Output section of the Environment Division contains
two paragraphs:
FILE-CONTROL paragraph
I-O-CONTROL paragraph.

The IBM® i system has four categories of files:
Database Files
Device Files
DDM Files
Distributed Files
Save Files.
