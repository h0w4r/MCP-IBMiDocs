The declarative TAG operation names the label that identifies the
destination of a GOTO (Go To) or CABxx (Compare and Branch) operation.
It can be specified anywhere within calculations, including within
total calculations.

A GOTO within a subroutine in the cycle-main
procedure can be issued to a TAG within the same subroutine, detail
calculations or total calculations. A GOTO within a subroutine in
a subprocedure can be issued to a TAG within the same subroutine,
or within the body of the subprocedure.

The control level entry (positions 7 and 8) can
be blank or can contain an L1 through L9 indicator, the LR indicator,
or the L0 entry to group the statement within the appropriate section
of the program. Conditioning indicator entries (positions 9 through
11) are not allowed.

Factor 1 must contain the name of the destination of a GOTO or
CABxx operation. This name must be a unique symbolic name, which
is specified in factor 2 of a GOTO operation or in the result field
of a CABxx operation. The name can be used as a common point for multiple
GOTO or CABxx operations.

Branching to the TAG from a different part of the RPG IV logic
cycle may result in an endless loop. For example, if a detail calculation
line specifies a GOTO operation to a total calculation TAG operation,
an endless loop may occur.
