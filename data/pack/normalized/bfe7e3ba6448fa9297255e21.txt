Opening Database and DDM Files as Record Files
To open an iSeries database file as a record file, use the
_Ropen() function with one of the following modes:

rr or rr+
wr or wr+
ar or ar+

The valid keyword parameters for database and DDM files are:

arrseq
blkrcd
commit
ccsid
dupkey
riofb
secure
varparm
vlr
rtncode
