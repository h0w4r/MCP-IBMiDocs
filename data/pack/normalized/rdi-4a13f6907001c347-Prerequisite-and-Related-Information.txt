Prerequisite and Related Information

Use the IBM® i
Information Center as
your starting point for looking
up IBM i technical
information. You can access the IBM i
Information Center from the following Web site:
https://www.ibm.com/docs/i.

The IBM i
Information Center
contains new and updated system information, such as software installation, Linux®, WebSphere®, Java™,
high availability, database, logical partitions, CL commands, and
system application programming interfaces (APIs). In addition, it
provides advisors and finders to assist in planning, troubleshooting,
and configuring your system hardware and software.
