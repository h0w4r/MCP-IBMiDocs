*CONTROL (*CBL) and the COPY Statement
A COPY statement bearing the SUPPRESS phrase overrides any *CONTROL or
*CBL options contained in the copied member, but the compiler remembers *CONTROL
and *CBL statements that appear in a suppressed COPY member. Once the COPY
member has been processed, the last NOSOURCE or SOURCE option in it runs.
If a COPY statement does not bear the SUPPRESS phrase, *CONTROL and *CBL
statements within the copied member run immediately.
