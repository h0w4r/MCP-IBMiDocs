Punctuators and operators
A punctuator is
a token that has syntactic and semantic meaning to the compiler, but
the exact significance depends on the context. A punctuator can also
be a token that is used in the syntax of the preprocessor.
C99 and C++ define the following tokens as punctuators,
operators, or preprocessing tokens:
