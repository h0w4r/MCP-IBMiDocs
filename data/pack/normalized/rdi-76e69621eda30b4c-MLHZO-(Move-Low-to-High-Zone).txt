The MLHZO operation moves the zone portion of a character from
the rightmost zone in factor 2 to the leftmost zone in the result
field. Factor 2 can be defined as a numeric field or as a character
field, but the result field must be a character field. For further
information on the MLHZO operation, see Move Zone Operations.
