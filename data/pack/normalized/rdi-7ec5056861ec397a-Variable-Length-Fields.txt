Variable-Length Fields
You can bring a variable-length field into your program if you
specify the CVTOPT(*VARCHAR) option on the CRTCBLMOD or CRTBNDCBL
command, or the VARCHAR option of the PROCESS statement. A variable-length
field that you extract from an externally-described file becomes a
fixed-length group item in your program.
See the IBM® Rational® Development Studio
for i: ILE COBOL Programmer's Guide for more detailed information
about these fields.
When you perform a WRITE operation before explicitly moving a record
to the record area, you will often write blanks, which have a hexadecimal
value of 40 (X'40'). For variable-length fields, this means that X'4040'
will be used as the current length of the field.
X'4040' translates to a decimal value of 16 448, which would probably
exceed the maximum defined length of the variable-length field. This
causes the WRITE operation or subsequent CLOSE operation to fail with
a file status of 90.
