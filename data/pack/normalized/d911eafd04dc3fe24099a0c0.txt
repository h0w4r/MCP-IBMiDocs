ACOS
The ACOS function returns a numeric value in radians that approximates
the arccosine of the argument specified.
The function type is numeric.

Format

>>-FUNCTION ACOS--(--argument-1--)-----------------------------><

argument-1
Must be class numeric. The value of argument-1 must be greater than
or equal to -1 and less than or equal to +1.

The returned value is the approximation of the arccosine of the argument,
and is greater than or equal to zero and less than or equal to Pi.
