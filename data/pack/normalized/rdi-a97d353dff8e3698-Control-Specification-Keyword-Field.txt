Control Specification Keyword Field

The rule for continuation on the control specification is:

The specification continues on or past position 7 of the next
control specification
Example

*.. 1 ...+... 2 ...+... 3 ...+... 4 ...+... 5 ...+... 6 ...+... 7 ...+... 8
