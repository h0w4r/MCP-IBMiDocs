The PICTURE character-string is a combination of G's and B's with at least
one of each.
Each G, and B represents a single DBCS character position (2 bytes).
USAGE DISPLAY-1 must be specified.
Associated VALUE clauses must specify a DBCS literal or the figurative
constant SPACE/SPACES.
