ADDRESS OF
ADDRESS OF refers to the calculated address of a data item. The data item
can be reference modified or subscripted. You may take the ADDRESS OF any
Data Division item that is
not a type-name or a subordinate of a type-name, and that does not have level
number 66 or 88. Such an address can be referenced, but not changed.
The ADDRESS OF an item is implicitly defined as USAGE IS POINTER.
