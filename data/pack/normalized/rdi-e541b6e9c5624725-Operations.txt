Operations

The RPG IV programming language allows you to do many different
types of operations on your data. To perform an operation, you use either
an operation code or a built-in function.

This chapter summarizes the operation codes and built-in functions
that are available. It also organizes the operation codes and built-in functions
into categories.

For detailed information about a specific operation code or built-in function,
see Operation Codes or Built-in Functions.
