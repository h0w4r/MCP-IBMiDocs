Job and Thread Breakpoints

There are two types of breakpoints: job and thread.

Typically, you use breakpoints to halt processing of a program, or
job.
Each thread in a threaded application may have it's
own thread breakpoint.

Both job and thread breakpoints can be either unconditional or
conditional.
In general, there is one set of debug commands and function keys for job
breakpoints and another for thread breakpoints.
For the rest of this section on breakpoints, the term breakpoint
refers to both job and thread, unless specifically mentioned otherwise.
