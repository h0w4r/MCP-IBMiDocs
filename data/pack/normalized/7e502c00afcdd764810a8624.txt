Change for CVTDAT with a 2-digit year format

Last Updated: 2025-10-07

In IBM® i 7.5, when the input or output format of the CVTDAT
command had a 2-digit year, the range of dates for the year portion of the date was always 1940 –
2039. The base year of the job was not considered.

In IBM i 7.6, the range of dates for dates with 2-digit
years depends on the base year of the job.

- If the base year of the job is 1940, the range is 1940 - 2039.

- If the base year of the job is 1970, the range is 1970 - 2069.
