SYSSEQUENCEAUTH
The SYSSEQUENCEAUTH view contains one row for every privilege
granted on a sequence. Note that this catalog view cannot be used
to determine whether a user is authorized to a sequence because the
privilege to use a sequence could be acquired through a group user
profile or special authority (such as *ALLOBJ).
