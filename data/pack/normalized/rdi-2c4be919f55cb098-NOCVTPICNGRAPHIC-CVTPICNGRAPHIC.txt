NOCVTPICNGRAPHIC / CVTPICNGRAPHIC
NOCVTPICNGRAPHIC indicates that the processing
of graphic fields depends upon the values specified for the CVTPICXGRAPHIC/NOCVTPICXGRAPHIC
and CVTPICGGRAPHIC/NOCVTPICGGRAPHIC options.
CVTPICNGRAPHIC indicates that fixed-length graphic
data items associated with CCSID 13488 or the CCSID defined by NTLCCSID
are declared as fixed-length N fields,
and are accessible to the ILE COBOL source program.
When the VARCHAR option
is also in use, variable-length graphic data items with CCSID 13488 or the CCSID defined by NTLCCSID
are declared as fixed-length group items (made of a numeric field followed by N type field),
and are accessible to the ILE COBOL source program.
