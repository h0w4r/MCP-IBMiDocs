If factor 1 is specified, factor 2 is subtracted from factor 1
and the difference is placed in the result field. If factor 1 is
not specified, the contents of factor 2 are subtracted from the contents
of the result field.

Factor 1 and factor 2 must be numeric, and each can contain one
of: an array, array element, field, figurative constant, literal,
named constant, subfield, or table name.

The result field must be numeric, and can contain one of: an array,
array element, subfield, or table name.

For rules for the SUB operation, see Arithmetic Operations.
