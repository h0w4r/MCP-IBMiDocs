System-Names
A system-name is a character-string that
is defined by IBM® to have a
specific meaning to the system. There are four types of system-names: computer-name
language-name
implementer-name (which includes environment-name and assignment-name)
locale-name

Computer-name can be written in DBCS characters, but the other
system-names cannot.
