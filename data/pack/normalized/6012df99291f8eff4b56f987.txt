Ending attribute character

The end of a field on the display is indicated by an ending attribute
character, unless there is only one space between that field and the next
field.

In that case, the beginning attribute character of the second field serves
as the ending attribute character of the first field. In any case, there must
always be at least one space left between fields in a record. When the last
position occupied by a field of a record is the last position in a line, the
ending attribute character for the field is in position 1 of the next line.
However, the next line is not considered part of the first record, and records
can be displayed on both lines at once.
