<xxcvt.h>
The <xxcvt.h> include file contains the
declarations that are used by the QXXDTOP, QXXDTOZ, QXXITOP, QXXITOZ, QXXPTOI, QXXPTOD, QXXZTOD,
and QXXZTOI conversion functions.
