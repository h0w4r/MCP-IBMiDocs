TRAILING-SIGN Phrase
The operational sign appears in the rightmost character position of the
field. This takes effect upon display of initial data in the data item and
also upon termination of the ACCEPT operation. This option affects only signed,
non-edited numeric data items. When this option is not specified, the sign
precedes the number.
