QUOTE / APOST
QUOTE specifies that the delimiter quotation
mark (") is used for nonnumeric literals and Boolean literals. This option
also specifies that the value of the figurative constant QUOTE has the EBCDIC
value of a quotation mark. QUOTE is the default value.
APOST specifies that the delimiter apostrophe (')
is used for nonnumeric literals and Boolean literals. This option also specifies
that the value of the figurative constant QUOTE has the EBCDIC value of an
apostrophe.
