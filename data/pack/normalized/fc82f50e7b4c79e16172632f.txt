Unary plus operator +
The + (unary plus) operator maintains the value of the
operand. The operand can have any arithmetic type or pointer type.
The result is not an lvalue.
The result has the same type as the operand after integral promotion.

Note:
Any plus sign in front of a constant is not part of
the constant.
