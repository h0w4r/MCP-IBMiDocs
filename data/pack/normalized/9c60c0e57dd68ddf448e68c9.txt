Implicit Scope Terminators
The separator period at the end of any sentence is an implicit scope terminator
that terminates the scope of any previous statement that is not yet terminated.
When a statement is contained within another statement, the next phrase of
the containing statement following the contained statement is an implicit
scope terminator that ends the scope of the contained statement.
A conditional statement not terminated by its scope terminator cannot be
contained within another statement.
Except for nesting conditional statements within IF statements, nested
statements must be imperative statements, and must follow the rules for imperative
statements. You should not nest conditional statements.
