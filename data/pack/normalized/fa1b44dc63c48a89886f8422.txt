ILE C/C++ Compiler Reference

Who Should Read This Book
This guide is for programmers who are familiar with
the C and C++ programming languages and who plan to use the ILE C/C++ compiler
to build new or maintain existing ILE C/C++ applications. You need experience
in using applicable IBM i menus and displays or Control Language
(CL) commands. You also need knowledge of ILE as explained in the ILE Concepts manual.
