Locales
A locale is a system object that specifies how language-specific data is
processed, printed and displayed. A locale is made up of categories
that describe the character set, collating sequence, date and time
representation and monetary representation of the language environment in
which it will be used. Using locales and locale-sensitive functions,
applications can be created that are independent of language, cultural data,
or character set, yet are sensitive to the language environment of the
user.
