Varying Identifiers
The way in which operands are increased or decreased depends on the number
of variables specified. In the following discussion, every reference to identifier-n
refers equally to index-name-n (except when identifier-n is the object of
the BY phrase).
If identifier-2 or identifier-5 is subscripted, the subscripts are evaluated
each time the content of the data item referenced by the identifier is set
or augmented. If identifier-3, identifier-4, identifier-6, or identifier-7
is subscripted, the subscripts are evaluated each time the content of the
data item referenced by the identifier is used in a setting or an augmenting
operation.
