Overlapping Operands
When a sending and a receiving item in any statement share a part or all
of their storage areas, yet are not defined by the same data description entry,
the result of the execution of such a statement is unpredictable. In addition,
the results are unpredictable for some statements in which sending and receiving
items are defined by the same data description entry. These cases are addressed
in the general rules associated with those statements.
