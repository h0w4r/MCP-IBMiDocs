Two decimal operands
If both operands are decimal, the operation is performed
in decimal. The result of any decimal arithmetic operation is a decimal number
with a precision and scale that are dependent on the operation and the precision
and scale of the operands. If the operation is addition or subtraction and
the operands do not have the same scale, the operation is performed with a
temporary copy of one of the operands. The copy of the shorter operand is
extended with trailing zeros so that its fractional part has the same number
of digits as the longer operand.
Unless specified otherwise, all functions and operations that
accept decimal numbers allow a precision of up to 63 digits. The result of
a decimal operation must not have a precision greater than 63.
