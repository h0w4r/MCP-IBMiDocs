NOCRTARKIDX/CRTARKIDX
NOCRTARKIDX specifies that temporary alternate record key (ARK) indexes
are not created if permanent ones cannot be found. NOCRTARKIDX is the default
value.
CRTARKIDX specifies that temporary alternate record key (ARK) indexes are
created if permanent ones cannot be found.
