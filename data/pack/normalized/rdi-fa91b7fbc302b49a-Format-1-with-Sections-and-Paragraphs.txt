Procedure Division - Format 1

>>-PROCEDURE DIVISION--+-----------------------------+---------->
    | .------------------. |   
    | V | |   
    '-USING----| Using-phrase |-+-'   

>--+-------------------------------------------------+--.------->
   | (1) |    
   '-+-RETURNING------+--+------------+--data-name-2-'    
    '----------------' '-ADDRESS OF-'    
    '-+------------+-'    
    | (1) |    
    '-GIVING-----'    

>--+------------------------------------------------------+----->
   | .----------------. |   
   | V | |   
   '-DECLARATIVES.----| Sections-2 |-+--END DECLARATIVES.-'   

   .----------------.   
   V |   
>----| Sections-1 |-+------------------------------------------><
