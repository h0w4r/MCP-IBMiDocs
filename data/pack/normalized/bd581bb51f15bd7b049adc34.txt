nosequence

nosequence syntax

>>-#--pragma--nosequence---------------------------------------><

Description
Specifies that the input record does not contain sequence numbers.
Notes® on
Usage
The #pragma nosequence directive takes effect on the line following
the directive and remains in effect until a #pragma sequence directive
is encountered or the end of the source member is reached.
See also pragma sequence.
