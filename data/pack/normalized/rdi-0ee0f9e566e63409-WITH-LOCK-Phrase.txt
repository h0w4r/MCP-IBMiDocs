WITH LOCK Phrase
While the ACCEPT statement is retrieving information into identifier-1,
the data area is locked with an LSRD (Lock Shared for Read) lock to prevent
the data area from being changed. After identifier-1 is accepted, the WITH
LOCK phrase places a LEAR (Lock Exclusive Allow Read) lock on the data area.
If a lock cannot be placed on the data area, an exception condition occurs.
To maintain a lock on the data area after the transfer of data, specify
this phrase. If a lock existed on the data area prior to this statement and
the statement did not contain a WITH LOCK phrase, the lock is released.
