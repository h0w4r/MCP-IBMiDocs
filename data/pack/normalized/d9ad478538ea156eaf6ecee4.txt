Relative Organization
Think of the file as a string of record areas, each of which contains a
single record. Each record area is identified by a relative record number;
the access method stores and retrieves a record, based on its relative record
number. For example, the first record area is addressed by relative record
number 1, and the 10th is addressed by relative record number 10. Relative
files must be assigned to DISK or DATABASE.
