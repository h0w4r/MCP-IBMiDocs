NOCRTF / CRTF
NOCRTF indicates that files that are
unavailable at the time of an OPEN operation are not created dynamically.
NOCRTF is the default value.
CRTF indicates that files that are unavailable at
the time of an OPEN operation are created dynamically.
Note: The
maximum record length for a file that will be created dynamically is 32 766.
