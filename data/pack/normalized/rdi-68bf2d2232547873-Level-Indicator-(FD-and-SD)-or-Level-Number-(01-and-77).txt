Level Indicator (FD and SD) or Level-Number (01 and 77)
A level indicator can be either FD or SD. It must begin in Area A and be followed by a space. (See File Section.) A level-number that must begin in Area A is a 1- or 2-digit integer with a value of 01 or 77. For more information, see Level-Numbers.
