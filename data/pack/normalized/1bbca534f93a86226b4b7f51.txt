Module Initialization

Module initialization occurs when the first procedure (either the
main procedure or a subprocedure) is called.

A cycle module has an additional form of initialization which can
occur repeatedly. Cycle-main procedure initialization occurs when
the cycle-main procedure is called the first time. It also occurs
on subsequent calls if the cycle-main procedure ended abnormally or
with LR on.
