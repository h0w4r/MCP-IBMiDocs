Initialization

To initialize the Timestamp field to the system date at runtime, specify
INZ(*SYS) on the definition specification. *SYS cannot be used with a field
that is exported. The Timestamp field can also be initialized at runtime to
a literal, named constant or figurative constant.
Note: Runtime
initialization takes place after static intitialization.
