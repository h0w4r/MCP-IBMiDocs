CHGDEVTAP UNLOAD change now allowed while device is varied on

Last Updated: 2025-11-20

With IBM® i 7.5 PTF MF70244, the Change Device Desc
(Tape) command (CHGDEVTAP) will now allow the UNLOAD
(Unload device at vary off) parameter to be modified while the device is varied on.
