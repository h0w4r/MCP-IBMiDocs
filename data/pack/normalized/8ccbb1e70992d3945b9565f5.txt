NOTIMESTAMP / TIMESTAMP
NOTIMESTAMP indicates that timestamp
data items are declared based on the DATETIME value specified in the ILE COBOL source
program. DATETIME can also be specified using the commands.
TIMESTAMP indicates that timestamp data items are
declared with their DDS names, and as timestamp data items of class date-time.
