Comparing DBCS Operands
The rules for comparing DBCS or DBCS-edited operands are the same as those
for the comparison of nonnumeric operands. The comparison is based on a binary
collating sequence of the hexadecimal values of the DBCS characters. The
PROGRAM COLLATING clause of the OBJECT-COMPUTER paragraph will have no effect
on this.
