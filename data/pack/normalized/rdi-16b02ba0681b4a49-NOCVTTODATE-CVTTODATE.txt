NOCVTTODATE / CVTTODATE
NOCVTTODATE indicates that DDS data types
with their DATFMT keyword (excluding the DDS date data type) are declared
in the ILE COBOL source program based on their original DDS type.
CVTTODATE indicates that DDS data types with their
DATFMT keyword (excluding the DDS date data type) are declared in the ILE COBOL source
program as date data types.
