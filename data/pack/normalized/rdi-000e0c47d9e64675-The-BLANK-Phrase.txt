The BLANK Phrase
BLANK is effective each time the screen item containing this clause is
displayed.
BLANK LINE erases from the current cursor position to the end of the current
line. BLANK SCREEN erases the entire screen and places the cursor at line
1, column 2.
The erasing is done before the item is displayed.
