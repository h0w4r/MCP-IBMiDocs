TAN
The TAN function returns a numeric value that approximates the tangent
of the angle or arc that is specified by the argument in radians.
The function type is numeric.

Format

>>-FUNCTION TAN--(--argument-1--)------------------------------><

argument-1
Must be class numeric.

The returned value is the approximation of the tangent of argument-1.
