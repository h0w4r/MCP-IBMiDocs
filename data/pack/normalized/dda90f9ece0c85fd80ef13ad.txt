Testing Conditions

Conditions are tested using /IF groups, consisting of an /IF directive,
followed by zero or more /ELSEIF directives, followed optionally by an /ELSE
directive, followed by an /ENDIF directive.

Any source lines except compile-time data, are valid between the directives
of an /IF group. This includes nested /IF groups.
Note: There is
no practical limit to the nesting level of /IF groups.

/IF Condition-Expression

/ELSEIF Condition-Expression

/ELSE

/ENDIF
