The Program Development Process
During the development process, an ILE program passes through five
stages:

Preparing
Compiling
Binding
Running
Debugging

These process steps are not necessarily performed in the order
listed. You can compile, correct compile-time errors, modify, and
recompile the program several times before binding it.
