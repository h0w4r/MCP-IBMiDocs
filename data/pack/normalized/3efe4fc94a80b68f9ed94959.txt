NOADJFILLER / ADJFILLER
NOADJFILLER If a pointer data item is
the first member of a group, any implicit fillers inserted by the compiler
to align this pointer data item are inserted immediately after the group.
This is the default.
ADJFILLER If a pointer data item is the first member
of a group, any implicit fillers inserted by the compiler to align this pointer
data item are inserted immediately before the group.
