Macro definition directives
Macro definition directives include the following directives and
operators:

The #define directive, which defines a macro
The #undef directive, which removes a macro definition
Standard predefined macros and macros that are predefined for IBM® i are described in "Predefined
macros" in the ILE C/C++ Compiler Reference.
