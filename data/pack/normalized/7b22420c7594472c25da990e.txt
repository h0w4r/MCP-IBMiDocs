Free-Form Specification

The rules for continuation on a free-form calculation specification
are:

The free-form line can be continued on the next line. The statement
continues until a semicolon is encountered.
Example

/FREE
time = hours * num_employees
+ overtime_saved;
/END-FREE

A continuation character is not used to continue a statement
on a subsequent line unless a name or a literal is being continued.
See Continuation Rules for more information about continuing names
and literals.

Continuation is not allowed for numeric literals.
Numeric literals must be specified on a single line in free-form.
