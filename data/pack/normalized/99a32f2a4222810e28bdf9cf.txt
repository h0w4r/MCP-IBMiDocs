Boolean

For a Boolean receiving item, only the first byte of the sending item
is moved.
If the sending item is alphanumeric, the first character of the sending
item is moved. The characters "0" and "1" are equivalent to the Boolean values
B"0" and B"1", respectively.
If the sending item is ZERO, it is treated as the Boolean literal B"0".
