Positions 50-63 (Result Field)

The result field names the field or record format that contains the result
of the calculation operation specified in positions 26 through 35. The field
specified must be modifiable. For example, it cannot be a lookahead field
or a user date field. With some operation codes, two operands may be specified
separated by a colon. See Operation Codes for the result field rules
for individual operation codes.
