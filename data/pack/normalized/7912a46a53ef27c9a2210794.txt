Alternate Collating Sequence

The alternate collating sequence applies only to single-byte character
data.

Each character is represented internally by a hexadecimal value, which
governs the order (ascending or descending sequence) of the characters and
is known as the normal collating sequence. The alternate collating sequence
function can be used to alter the normal collating sequence. This function
also can be used to allow two or more characters to be considered equal.
