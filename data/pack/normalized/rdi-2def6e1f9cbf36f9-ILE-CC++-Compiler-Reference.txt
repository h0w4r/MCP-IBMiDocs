pagesize

pagesize syntax

>>-#--pragma--pagesize--(--+---+--)----------------------------><
'-n-'

Description
Sets the number of lines per page to n for
the generated source listing. The pagesize pragma may not affect the
option listing page (sometimes called the Prolog).
