LINKPGM / LINKPRC
LINKPGM indicates that the linkage type
for external CALL/CANCEL 'literal' form target is a program object. LINKPGM
is the default value.
LINKPRC indicates that the linkage type for external
CALL/CANCEL 'literal' form target is an ILE procedure.

Note: You may override this option for specific external CALL/CANCEL
'literal' target lists by specifying the following sentence in the SPECIAL-NAMES
paragraph:
LINKAGE TYPE IS implementer-name FOR target-list.
