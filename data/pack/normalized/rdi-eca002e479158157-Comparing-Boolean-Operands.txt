Comparing Boolean Operands
Boolean operands are used only in the [NOT] EQUAL TO relation
condition. Boolean operands cannot be compared to non-Boolean operands. Boolean
data items and literals must be one position in length. Two Boolean operands
are equal if they both have a value of Boolean 1 or Boolean 0.
