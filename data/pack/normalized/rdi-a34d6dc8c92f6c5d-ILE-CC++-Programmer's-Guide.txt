Handling Extra Precision for Multiplication and Division
The _DecimalT class template allows a maximum of 62 and
93 digits as the internal results for the multiplication and division
operations respectively. This is different from the ILE C packed
decimal data type in which a maximum of 31 digits is used for both
operations.

Note:This internal result is different from the intermediate result. The
internal result is designated to store the temporary result during the
operation. After the operation is completed, the internal result is
converted to the intermediate result and returned to the caller.
