Free-Form Syntax

See Free-Form Statements for information on
the columns available for free-form statements.

The control-specification keywords are used to determine how the program
will deal with devices and how certain types of information will be represented.

The control-specification keywords also include compile-option keywords
that override the default or specified options on the CRTBNDRPG and CRTRPGMOD
commands. These keywords determine the compile options to be used on every
compile of the program.
