SIN
The SIN function returns a numeric value that approximates the sine of
the angle or arc specified by the argument in radians.
The function type is numeric.

Format

>>-FUNCTION SIN--(--argument-1--)------------------------------><

argument-1
Must be class numeric.

The returned value is the approximation of the sine of argument-1 and is
greater than or equal to -1 and less than or equal to +1.
