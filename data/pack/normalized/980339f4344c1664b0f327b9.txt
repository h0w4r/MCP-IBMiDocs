ORGANIZATION IS SEQUENTIAL (Format 1)

ORGANIZATION Clause - Sequential Files

>>-+----------------------+--SEQUENTIAL------------------------><
   '-ORGANIZATION--+----+-'    
    '-IS-'    

A predecessor-successor relationship of the records in the files is established
by the order in which records are placed in the file when it is created or
extended (arrival sequence access path).
