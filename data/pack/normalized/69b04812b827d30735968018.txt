RESERVE Clause
The RESERVE clause reserves input-output areas. It is syntax checked, but
treated as documentation.

RESERVE Clause - Format

    (1)    
>>-RESERVE------integer--+-------+-----------------------------><
    +-AREA--+   
    '-AREAS-'   

Notes:Syntax-checked only.
