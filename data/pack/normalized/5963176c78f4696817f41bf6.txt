Statement
A statement is a syntactically valid combination
of identifiers and symbols
(literals, relational-operators, and so forth) beginning with a COBOL verb.
Execution begins with the first statement in the Procedure Division, excluding
declaratives. Statements are executed in the order in which they are presented
for compilation, unless the statement rules dictate some other order of execution.
The Procedure Division ends at the physical end of the program; that is,
the physical position in a source program after which no further statements
appear.
