Data Organization and Access Modes
Data organization is the permanent logical structure
of the file. You tell the computer how to retrieve records from the file by
specifying the access mode. In COBOL you can specify
any of four types of data organization, and three access modes. Sequentially
organized data may only be accessed sequentially; however, data that has indexed
or relative organization may be accessed with any of the three access methods.
