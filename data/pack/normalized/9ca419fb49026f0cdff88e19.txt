Procedures
Within the Procedure Division, a procedure consists
of:
A section or a group of sections
A paragraph or group of paragraphs.

Note: A COBOL procedure should not be confused with an ILE
procedure (an ILE COBOL source program).

A procedure-name is a user-defined name that identifies
a section or a paragraph.
