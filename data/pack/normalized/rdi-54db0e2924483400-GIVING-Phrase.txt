GIVING Phrase
The data item referenced by the identifier that follows the word
GIVING is set to the calculated result of the arithmetic operation. Because
this identifier is not involved in the computation, it may be a numeric-edited
item.

Giving Phrase - Format

    .---------------------------.   
    V |   
>>-GIVING----identifier-1--+---------+-+-----------------------><
    '-ROUNDED-'
