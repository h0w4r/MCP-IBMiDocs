SQL limits
The following tables describe certain SQL and database
limits imposed by the DB2® for
i database
manager.
Note: System storage limits may preclude the limits specified here.
For example, see Maximum row sizes.
A limit of storage means that the limit
is dependent on the amount of storage available.
A limit of statement means that the
limit is dependent on the limit for the maximum length of a statement.
