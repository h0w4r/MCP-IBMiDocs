COMPUTATIONAL-1 or COMP-1 Phrase
The COMPUTATIONAL-1 or COMP-1 phrase is specified for internal floating-point
items (single precision). COMP-1 items are 4 bytes long. The sign is contained
in the first bit of the leftmost byte and the exponent is contained in the
next 8 bits. The last 23 bits contain the mantissa. For conditional expressions,
the class condition cannot be used for COMP-1 or COMPUTATIONAL-1 internal
floating-point data items.
