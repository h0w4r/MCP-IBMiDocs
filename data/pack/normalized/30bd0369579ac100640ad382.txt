FORMAT Clause and PICTURE CLAUSE Similarities
A FORMAT clause defines an implicit PICTURE
clause. Although there is no PICTURE character-string that can easily describe
a date or time item, for some formats, an approximate definition does exist.
For example, a date item with a FORMAT '%y,%m,%d' is similar to the PICTURE
99/99/99, where the '/' PICTURE symbol is replaced with a ','.
