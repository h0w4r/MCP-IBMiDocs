SUPPRESS Phrase
The SUPPRESS phrase causes a COPY statement to suppress the listing of
copied statements. For its duration, this type of COPY statement overrides
any *CONTROL or *CBL statement.
If the copied member contains *CONTROL or *CBL statements, the last one
runs once the COPY member has been processed.
For a nested COPY statement, SUPPRESS is in effect only until the next
COPY is encountered. Suppression resumes after the nested COPY is completed.
