AUTO (Auto) keyword for display files

Under some circumstances, the AUTO keyword is equivalent to the
CHECK keyword.

The AUTO keyword is equivalent to the CHECK keyword as follows:

AUTO(RA)

CHECK(ER)

AUTO(RAB)

CHECK(RB)

AUTO(RAZ)

CHECK(RZ)

The format of the keyword is: AUTO(RA [RAB | RAZ]) AUTO(RAB | RAZ)
The
CHECK keyword is preferred.
