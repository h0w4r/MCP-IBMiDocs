Data Division Overview
The Data Division of a COBOL source program describes, in a structured
manner, all the data to be processed by the object program; also the relationship
between physical and logical records. The Data Division is optional in a COBOL
source program.
This section outlines the structure of the Data Division and explains the
types of data.
