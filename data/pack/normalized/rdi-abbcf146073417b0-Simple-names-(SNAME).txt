Simple names (*SNAME)
Simple names are used for control language (CL)
variables, labels, and keywords to simplify the syntax of CL. Simple names
are the same as unquoted basic names but with one exception: periods (.) cannot
be used.
Some examples of simple names are as follows:
NEWCMD LIB_2
