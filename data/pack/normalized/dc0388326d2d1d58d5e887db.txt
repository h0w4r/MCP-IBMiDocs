/EJECT

Last Updated: 2025-10-07

See Compiler Directives for information on
the columns available for directives.

/EJECT must be followed by at least two blanks.
The remaining columns can contain comments.

/EJECT cannot be coded within a free-form statement.

Use /EJECT to indicate that subsequent specifications
are to begin on a new page of the compiler listing.
If the spool file is already at the top of a new page, /EJECT
will not advance to a new page.
/EJECT is not printed on the compiler listing.
