Comparison of Numeric and Nonnumeric Operands
Rules for numeric and nonnumeric comparisons are given in the following
tables. If either of the operands is a group item, nonnumeric comparison
rules apply.
Table 1 summarizes permissible
comparisons with nonnumeric operands.
Table 2 summarizes permissible
comparisons with numeric operands.
The symbols used in Table 1 and Table 2 are as follows: NN = Comparison for nonnumeric operands.
NU = Comparison for numeric operands.
NL = Comparison for national operands.
NLN = Comparison for national and nonnational operands.
DT = Comparison for date-time operands.
Blank = Comparison is not allowed.
