Authority for libraries specification
Certain authorities can be given to users for libraries.

Object authority
The types of object authority include object operational authority,
object management authority, and object existence authority.
Data authority
The types of data authority include add authority, read authority,
update authority, execute authority, and delete authority.
Combined authority
The types of combined authority include *USE authority, *CHANGE
authority, *ALL authority, and *EXCLUDE authority.
