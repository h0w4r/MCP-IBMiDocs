Conditions Relating to the Environment

*ILERPG

This condition is defined if your program is being compiled by the ILE
RPG IV compiler (the compiler described in this document).
* This module is to be defined on different platforms. With
* the ILE RPG compiler, the BNDDIR keyword is used to
* indicate where procedures can be found. With a different
* compiler, the BNDDIR keyword might not be valid.
/IF DEFINED(*ILERPG)
H BNDDIR('QC2LE')
/ENDIF To learn what conditions are available with another
version of the RPG IV compiler, consult the reference for the compiler.
