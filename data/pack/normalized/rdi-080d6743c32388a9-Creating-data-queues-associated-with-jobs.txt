Creating data queues associated with jobs
You can associate a data queue with exit point QIBM_QWT_JOBNOTIFY
to place an entry on a data queue for certain job transitions.
An entry can be placed on a data queue when a job is placed
on a job queue, when a job starts or a when a job ends. See the Job Notification
Exit Point (QIBM_QWT_JOBNOTIFY) for details on how to create
the data queue and the format of the information placed on the data
queue.
