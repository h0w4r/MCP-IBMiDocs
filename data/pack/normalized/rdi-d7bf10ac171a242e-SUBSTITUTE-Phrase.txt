SUBSTITUTE Phrase

SUBSTITUTE Phrase - Format

>>-SUBSTITUTE--+----------------------------+------------------><
    '-ALL--+-------------------+-'   
    '-EXCEPT--literal-2-'    

The SUBSTITUTE phrase allows you to
bring DDS into your program, while preserving certain characters, such as
the underscore character. The underscore is not a standard ILE COBOL character,
but it is required for specifying locale categories. To preserve the underscore
character, for example, in copied DDS, the SUBSTITUTE phrase would be used
as follows:
…SUBSTITUTE ALL EXCEPT '_'.

literal-2
Should be a 1-byte non-numeric literal. The character specified in literal-2
is not substituted.
