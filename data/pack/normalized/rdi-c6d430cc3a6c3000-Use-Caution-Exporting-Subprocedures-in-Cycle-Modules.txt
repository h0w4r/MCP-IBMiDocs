Use Caution Exporting Subprocedures in Cycle
Modules

If a module contains both a cycle-main procedure
and exported subprocedures, take great care to ensure that the RPG
cycle in the cycle-main procedure does not adversely affect
the global data, files, and data areas that the subprocedures are
using.

You must be aware of when files are opened and closed implicitly,
when data areas are locked and unlocked implicitly, and when global
data is initialized or re-initialized.
