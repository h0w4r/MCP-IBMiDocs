SYSTABLEINDEXSTAT
The SYSTABLEINDEXSTAT view contains one row for every index
that has at least one partition or member built over a table. If the
index is over more than one partition or member, the statistics include
all those partitions and members. If the table is a distributed table,
the partitions that reside on other database nodes are not included.
They are contained in the catalog views of the other database nodes.

view:
