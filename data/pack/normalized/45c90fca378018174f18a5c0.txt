nomargins

nomargins syntax

>>-#--pragma--nomargins----------------------------------------><

Description
Specifies that the entire input record is to be scanned for input.
Notes® on
Usage
The #pragma nomargins directive takes effect on the line following
the directive and remains in effect until a #pragma margins directive
is encountered or the end of the source member is reached.
See also pragma margins.
