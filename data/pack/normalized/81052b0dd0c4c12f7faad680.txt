Special Considerations for FORMATFILEs
The keywords END-OF-PAGE and EOP are equivalent. When the END-OF-PAGE
phrase is specified, and an EOP condition exists after the processing of the
WRITE statement for the FORMATFILE file, the END-OF-PAGE imperative statement
is processed. An EOP condition for a FORMATFILE file occurs when the logical
end of page is reached during the processing of a WRITE statement for that
file. The logical end of the printed page is specified in the overflow line
number parameter of the CRTPRTF command or the OVRPRTF command.
