Considerations Regarding Use of REPLACING in Format 2 COPY
Statement
The REPLACING phrase can be used to replace any of the generated
COBOL source, including the level numbers. (See REPLACING Phrase for additional information.) You
should, however, note the following exception: When RECORD KEY IS EXTERNALLY-DESCRIBED-KEY is specified, the
REPLACING phrase cannot change a format-name or the name of a field
that is a key.
