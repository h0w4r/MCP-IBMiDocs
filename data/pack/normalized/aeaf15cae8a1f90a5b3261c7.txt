PRTCORR / NOPRTCORR
PRTCORR indicates that the compiler inserts
comment lines in the compiler listing indicating which elementary items were
included as a result of the use of the CORRESPONDING phrase. PRTCORR is the
default value.
NOPRTCORR indicates that the compiler does not insert
comment lines in the compiler listing when the CORRESPONDING phrase is used.
