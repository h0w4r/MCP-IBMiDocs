Assignments to LOB locators
When a LOB locator is used, it can refer to any string data. If
a LOB locator is used for the first fetch of a cursor and the cursor is on
a remote server, LOB locators must be used for all subsequent fetches unless
the *NOOPTLOB precompile option is used.
