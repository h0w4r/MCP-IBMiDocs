REMAINDER Phrase
The result of subtracting the product of the quotient and the divisor from
the dividend is stored in identifier-4. If identifier-3, the quotient, is
a numeric-edited item, the quotient used to calculate the remainder is an
intermediate field that contains the unedited quotient.
IBM ExtensionIBM Extension The REMAINDER phrase is not valid if the receiver or any
of the operands are floating-point items. End of IBM Extension
End of IBM Extension Any subscripts for identifier-4 in the REMAINDER phrase are evaluated after
the result of the divide operation is stored in identifier-3 of the GIVING
phrase.
