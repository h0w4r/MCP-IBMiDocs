Predisplaying by Data Type
In the absence of the UPDATE phrase, you can control the predisplaying
of some data. To predisplay only numeric-edited data, specify the *ACCUPDNE
option of the EXTDSPOPT parameter. To predisplay all data, use the default
option, *ACCUPDALL.
