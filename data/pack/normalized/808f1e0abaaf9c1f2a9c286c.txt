Date, Time, and Timestamp
For class date-time items with a
USAGE of DISPLAY, data is aligned at the leftmost character position, and
(if necessary) padded with spaces to the right.
For class date-time items with a USAGE of PACKED-DECIMAL, data is aligned
at the rightmost digit position, and (if necessary) padded with zeros to the
left.
