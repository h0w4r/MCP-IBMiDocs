Note:
At least one resulting indicator is required.

The %FOUND built-in function can be used as an alternative to specifying
an NR or FD resulting indicator.

You must specify factor 2 or the result field. You may specify both.

You must specify factor 1 or the result field. You may specify both.

The %EOF built-in function can be used as an alternative to specifying
an EOF or BOF resulting indicator.

The %EQUAL built-in function can be used to test the SETLL and LOOKUP
operations.

For all operation codes with extender 'E', either the extender 'E' or
an ER error indicator can be specified, but not both.

You must specify the extender 'E' or an error indicator for the TEST operation.
