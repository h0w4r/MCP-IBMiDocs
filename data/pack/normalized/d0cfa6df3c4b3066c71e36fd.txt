END-STRING Phrase
This explicit scope terminator serves to delimit the scope of the STRING
statement. END-STRING permits a conditional STRING statement to be nested
in another conditional statement. END-STRING may also be used with an imperative
STRING statement.
For more information, see Delimited Scope Statements.
