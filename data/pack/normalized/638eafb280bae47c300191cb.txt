Computational Items
A computational item is a value used in arithmetic operations. Computational
items must be numeric. These include binary, packed-decimal, and internal
floating point data items.
If the USAGE of a group item is described with any of these items, the
elementary items within the group have this usage. The group itself is considered
nonnumeric and cannot be used in numeric operations, except for those using
the CORRESPONDING phrase (see CORRESPONDING Phrase).
The maximum length of a computational item is 18 decimal digits.
IBM ExtensionIBM Extension The maximum length of a packed-decimal computational item is 31 decimal
digits. End of IBM Extension
