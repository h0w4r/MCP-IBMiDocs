Error Conditions
If incompatible options are used with the RTBND(*LLP64) option, diagnostic
error CZS2121 is generated and the compilation is stopped.
If the RTBND(*LLP64) option is used with any release prior Version
5.3, diagnostic error CZS2120 is generated and the compilation is
stopped.
