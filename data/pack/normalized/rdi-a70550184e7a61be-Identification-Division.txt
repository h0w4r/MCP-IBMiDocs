Identification Division
The Identification Division must be the first division in every
COBOL source program. It names the program and may include the date
the program was written, the date of compilation, and other such documentary
information about the program.

Identification Division - Format

>>-+-IDENTIFICATION-+--DIVISION.--PROGRAM-ID.------------------->
   | (1) |    
   '-ID-------------'    

>--+-program-name-+--------------------------------------------->
   | (1) |   
   '-literal------'   

>--+--------------------------------------------------------+--->
   | (2) |   
   '-+----+--+-COMMON------+-----------+-----+--+---------+-'   
    '-IS-' | +-INITIAL---+ | '-PROGRAM-'    
    | '-RECURSIVE-' |    
    '-+-INITIAL---+--+------------+-'    
    '-RECURSIVE-' | (2) |    
    '-COMMON-----'    

>--.--+--------------------------------+------------------------>
    '-AUTHOR.--+-------------------+-'   
    | .---------------. |    
    | V | |    
    '---comment-entry-+-'    

>--+--------------------------------------+--------------------->
