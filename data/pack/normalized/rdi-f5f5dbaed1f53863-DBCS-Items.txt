DBCS Items
DBCS data items and literals can be used with all relational operators.
Comparisons (between two DBCS items only) are based on the binary collating
sequence of the hexadecimal values of the DBCS characters. If the items are
not of the same length, the smaller item is padded with DBCS spaces to the
right.
