Factor 2 is subtracted from a field of zeros. The difference,
which is the negative of factor 2, is placed in the result field.
You can use the operation to change the sign of a field. Factor 1
is not used. Factor 2 must be numeric and can contain one of the following:
an array, array element, field, figurative constant, literal, named
constant, subfield, or table name.

The result field must be numeric, and can contain one of the following:
an array, array element, field, subfield, or table name.

Half-adjust can be specified.

For the rules for the Z-SUB operation, see Arithmetic Operations.
