SOURCE-COMPUTER Paragraph
The SOURCE-COMPUTER paragraph describes the computer on which the source
program is to be compiled.

SOURCE-COMPUTER Paragraph - Format

>>-SOURCE-COMPUTER.--------------------------------------------->

>--+------------------------------------------------+----------><
   '-computer-name--+--------------------------+--.-'   
    '-+------+--DEBUGGING MODE-'    
    '-WITH-'    

computer-name
