Reserved schema names
This is the list of reserved schema names.
The following schema names are reserved:
QSYS2
SYSCAT
SYSFUN
SYSIBM
SYSIBMADM
SYSPROC
SYSPUBLIC
SYSSTAT
SYSTEM

In addition, it is strongly recommended that schema names
never begin with the Q prefix or SYS prefix, as Q and SYS are by convention
used to indicate an area reserved by the system.
It is also recommended not to use SESSION as a schema
name.
