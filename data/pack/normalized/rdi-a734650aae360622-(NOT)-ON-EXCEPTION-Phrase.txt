(NOT) ON EXCEPTION Phrase
If an error occurs while accessing the data-area, then any imperative statement
specified in the ON EXCEPTION phrase is processed. In the absence of the
ON EXCEPTION phrase, a run-time message is issued. If the data area is accessed
successfully, any imperative statement specified in the NOT ON EXCEPTION phrase
is processed.
