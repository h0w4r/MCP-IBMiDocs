Special member functions (C++ only)
Special member functions are used to create,
destroy, initialize, convert, and copy class objects. These include
the following:

Constructors
Destructors
Conversion constructors
Conversion functions
Copy constructors
For full descriptions of these functions, see Special member functions (C++ only).
