LEN(length)

The LEN keyword is used to define the length in characters of a Data Structure or
character, UCS-2 or graphic definition.
It is valid for Data Structure definitions, and for Prototype, Prototyped Parameter,
Standalone Field and
Subfield definitions where the type entry is A (Alphanumeric),
C® (UCS-2), or G (Graphic).

Note: In free-form definitions,
the LEN keyword is only used for
data-structure definitions.
For other definition types, the length of the
item is specified as a parameter for the
data-type keyword.
