Parsing System Built-In Names

When the literal or named constant specified on a bound call starts with
"CEE" or an underscore ('_'), the compiler will treat this as a system built-in.
(A bound call results with either CALLB or with a prototyped call where EXTPGM
is not specfied on the prototype).

If it is not actually a system built-in, then a warning will appear in
the listing; you can ignore this warning.

For more information on APIs, see the IBM® i
Information Center programming
category. To avoid confusion with system provided APIs, you should not
name your procedures starting with "CEE".
