SOURCE / SRC / NOSOURCE / NOSRC
SOURCE or SRC indicates that the compiler
produces a source listing, consisting of the ILE COBOL source program and all
compile-time error messages. SOURCE or SRC is the default value.
NOSOURCE or NOSRC indicates that the compiler does
not produce the source part of the listing. If you do not require a source
listing, you should use this option because compilation may take less time.
