Class members and friends (C++ only)
This section discusses the declaration of class members with respect
to the information hiding mechanism and how a class can grant functions
and classes access to its nonpublic members by the use of the friend
mechanism. C++ expands the concept of information hiding to include
the notion of having a public class interface but a private implementation.
It is the mechanism for limiting direct access to the internal representation
of a class type by functions in a program.
