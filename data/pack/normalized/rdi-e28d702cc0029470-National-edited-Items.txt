The PICTURE character-string can contain the following symbols:
N B 0 /

The string must contain at least one N and at least one B, zero (0), or slash (/).
The contents of the item in standard data format may be any allowable character from the UCS-2
character set.
USAGE NATIONAL must be specified or implied.
Any associated VALUE clause must specify a nonnumeric literal or a figurative constant.
The literal is treated exactly as specified; no editing is done.
