Numeric-edited
The data is aligned on the decimal point, and (if necessary) truncated
or padded with zeros at either end, except when editing causes replacement
of leading zeros.
However, if the LOCALE phrase of the PICTURE clause is specified in its
data description entry, alignment and zero-fill or truncation takes place
as described in LOCALE Phrase.
