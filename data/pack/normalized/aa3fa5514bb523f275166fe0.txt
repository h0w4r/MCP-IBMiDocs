ATAN
The ATAN function returns a numeric value in radians that approximates
the arctangent of the argument specified.
The function type is numeric.

Format

>>-FUNCTION ATAN--(--argument-1--)-----------------------------><

argument-1
Must be class numeric.

The returned value is the approximation of the arctangent of argument-1,
and is greater than -Pi/2 and less than +Pi/2.
