The PREFIX Options
The PREFIX options allows you to specify a prefix (literal) to be inserted in front of every field name. You can use it
to help identify (that is, document) the contents or usage of the field. The
literal can be contained within a pair of apostrophes or a pair of quotation
marks. The maximum length of the literal allowed is 15 characters.
