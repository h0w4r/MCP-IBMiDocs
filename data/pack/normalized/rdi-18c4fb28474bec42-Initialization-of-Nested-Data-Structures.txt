Initialization of Nested Data Structures

The keyword INZ(*LIKEDS) is allowed on a LIKEDS subfield. The LIKEDS
subfield is initialized exactly the same as the corresponding data
structure.

Keyword INZ is allowed on a LIKEDS subfield. All nested subfields
of the LIKEDS subfield are initialized to their default values.

If keyword INZ is coded on a main data structure definition, keyword
INZ is implied on all subfields of the data structure without explicit
initialization. This includes LIKEDS subfields.
