NOCOMPRESSDBG/COMPRESSDBG
NOCOMPRESSDBG indicates the debug listing view will not be compressed by
the compiler. NOCOMPRESSDBG is the default value.
COMPRESSDBG indicates the debug listing view will be compressed by the
compiler.
