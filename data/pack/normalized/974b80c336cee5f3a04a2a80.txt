NOVARCHAR / VARCHAR
NOVARCHAR indicates that variable-length
fields are ignored, and are declared as FILLER fields. NOVARCHAR is the default
value.
VARCHAR indicates that variable-length fields are
declared as fixed-length group items, and are accessible to the COBOL source
program.
