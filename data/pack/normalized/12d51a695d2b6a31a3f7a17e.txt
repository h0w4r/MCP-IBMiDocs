Entradas técnicas
- MODULE: Compiled object that is bound into a program or service program; it is not normally invoked as a standalone callable program.
- CRTRPGMOD: Creates an RPG module object.
- CRTPGM: Creates/binds a program from modules and service programs.
- CRTSRVPGM: Creates a service program from modules.
- Service program: ILE object containing callable procedures exported for reuse by programs or other service programs.
- Binding directory: Object listing modules and service programs to search during binding.
- Binder language: Defines exports and signature behavior for service programs.
- Signature: Compatibility token used by service programs to validate expected exports at bind/runtime.
- EXPORT: Makes a procedure or symbol available from a module/service program.
- CALLP: Calls a prototyped procedure, including procedures exported by service programs.
- Activation group: ILE runtime container controlling activation and resource lifetime.
