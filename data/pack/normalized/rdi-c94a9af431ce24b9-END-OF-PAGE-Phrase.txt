END-OF-PAGE Phrase
When this phrase is specified (and the FD entry for this file contains
a LINAGE clause), and the logical end of the printed page is reached during
execution of the WRITE statement, the imperative-statement is executed.
If an END-OF-PAGE condition does not exist after the processing of a WRITE
statement with the NOT AT END-OF-PAGE phrase, control transfers to the imperative
statement associated with that phrase.
