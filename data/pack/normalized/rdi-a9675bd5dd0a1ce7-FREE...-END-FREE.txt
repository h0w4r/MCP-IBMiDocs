/FREE... /END-FREE

In earlier releases, the /FREE compiler directive
specified the beginning of a free-form calculation
specifications block. /END-FREE specified the end of the block.

If you code the /FREE or /END-FREE directive,
it will be ignored, but the syntax of the directive will be checked.
The columns following the directive must be blank.
See Free-Form Statements for information on
free-form statements.
