END-SEARCH Phrase
This explicit scope terminator serves to delimit the scope of the SEARCH
statement. END-SEARCH permits a conditional SEARCH statement to be nested
in another conditional statement. If the END-SEARCH phrase is specified, the
NEXT SENTENCE phrase must not be specified.
For more information, see Delimited Scope Statements.
