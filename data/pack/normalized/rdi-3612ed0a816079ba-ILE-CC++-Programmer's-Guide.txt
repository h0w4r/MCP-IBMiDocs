ILE C/C++ Programmer's Guide
Resource Allocation

An activation group is the key element in governing an ILE
program's resources and behavior. You can scope commitment-control
operations to the activation group level. You can scope file overrides
and shared open data paths to the activation group of the running
program. The behavior of a program upon termination is affected by the
activation group in which the program runs.
