Prototype Names

A prototype name is a symbolic name assigned to a prototype definition.
This name must be used when calling a prototyped procedure or program.
A prototype maybe explicitly specified, or it may be implicitly generated
by the compiler from the procedure interface when the procedure is defined
in the same module as the call.
