NOFIPS / MINIMUM / INTERMEDIATE / HIGH
NOFIPS indicates that the COBOL source
program is not FIPS flagged. NOFIPS is the default value.
MINIMUM indicates FIPS flag for minimum subset and
higher.
INTERMEDIATE indicates FIPS flag for intermediate
subset and higher.
HIGH indicates FIPS flag for high subset.
