%MINUTES (Number of Minutes)

%MINUTES(number)
%MINUTES converts a number into a duration that can be added to
a time or timestamp value.

%MINUTES can only follow the plus or minus sign in an addition or subtraction expression.
The value before the plus or minus sign must be a time or timestamp. The result
is a time or timestamp value with the appropriate number of minutes
added or subtracted. For a time, the resulting value is in *ISO format.
