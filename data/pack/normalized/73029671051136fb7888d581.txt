NTLPADCHAR(a b c)
This option specifies padding characters for the MOVE statement, when a
national data item receives single-byte, double-byte, or national characters.
Specify the padding characters in the following order:
Single-byte to national
The sending item is a single-byte item, such
as alphabetic or alphanumeric. Specify a national hexadecimal character. The
default is NX"0020".

Double-byte to national
The sending item is a double-byte item. Specify
a national hexadecimal character. The default is NX"3000".

National to national
The sending item is a national item. Specify a
national hexadecimal character. The default is NX"3000".
