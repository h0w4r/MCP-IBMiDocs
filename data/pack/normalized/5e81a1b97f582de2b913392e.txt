SECURE (NO-ECHO) Phrase
Operator-keyed data is prevented from appearing on the screen. This phrase
may be specified on a group screen item, in which case it applies to all suitable
elementary items which are subordinate to that item. When the SECURE phrase
is specified, only spaces and cursor appear in the screen item.
SECURE and NO-ECHO may be used interchangeably.
