Data type or keyboard shift (position 35)

You must type an O in this position
to make a field a DBCS-open field.

You can use DBCS and alphanumeric data in the same field. Use shift-control
characters to distinguish DBCS from alphanumeric data.

Type a G in this position to make a field a DBCS-graphic field.
