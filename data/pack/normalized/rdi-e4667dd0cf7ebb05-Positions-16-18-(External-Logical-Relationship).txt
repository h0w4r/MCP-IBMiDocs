Positions 16-18 (External Logical Relationship)

Entry
Explanation

AND or OR

AND⁄OR indicates a relationship between lines of output
indicators. AND⁄OR lines are valid for output records, but not
for fields.

See Positions 16-18 (Program-described Logical Relationship) for more information.
