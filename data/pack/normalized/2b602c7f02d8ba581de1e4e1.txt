INDICATORS Phrase
Specifies which indicators are to be written when a data record
is read. Indicators can be used to pass information about the data
record and how it was entered into the program.
For detailed information on the INDICATORS phrase, refer to the IBM® Rational® Development Studio
for i: ILE COBOL Programmer's Guide.
Identifier-3 must be either an elementary Boolean data item specified
without the OCCURS clause or a group item that has elementary Boolean
data items subordinate to it.
