Conversion between Character, Graphic and UCS-2 Data

Note: If graphic CCSIDs are ignored (CCSID(*GRAPH:*IGNORE) is specified
on a control statement or neither CCSID(*GRAPH) nor CCSID(*EXACT) is specified), graphic
data is not considered to have a CCSID and conversions are not supported between
graphic data and UCS-2 data or alphanumeric
data that has a CCSID other than the job CCSID.

Character, graphic, and UCS-2 data can have different CCSIDs (Coded Character
Set IDs). Conversion between these data types depends on the CCSID of the
data.
See CCSID control keyword
and CCSID definition keyword for more information.
