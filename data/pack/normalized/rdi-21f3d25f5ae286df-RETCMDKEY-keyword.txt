RETCMDKEY keyword

RETCMDKEY indicates whether to retain CAnn or
CFnn keys when the record is displayed.

You cannot specify the CAnn or CFnn keywords
with the RETCMDKEY on the file level or on this record format. You cannot
specify any CAnn, CFnn,
SFLDROP, SFLENTER, or SFLFOLD keywords on the record being defined.
