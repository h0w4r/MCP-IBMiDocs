Performance
Unless you specify the EXTDSPOPT(*NODFRWRT) parameter (no deferred writing)
in the CRTCBLMOD or CRTBNDCBL command, the ILE COBOL compiler buffers all extended
DISPLAY statements until the next ACCEPT statement is encountered. While the
*NODFRWRT option allows you to associate data errors with the statements that
cause them by performing DISPLAY statements as they are encountered, the deferred
writing (*DFRWRT) option improves performance by buffering data streams generated
by consecutive DISPLAY statements.
