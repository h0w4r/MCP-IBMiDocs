ON EXCEPTION Phrases
If ON EXCEPTION is specified, imperative-statement-1 is executed if the
ACCEPT operation finishes with anything other than a normal completion. That
is, if the CRT Status Key 1 is other than 0.
The use of the ON EXCEPTION phrase does not prevent the generation of a
run-time message for such conditions as workstation boundaries or out-of-screen
ranges.
If NOT ON EXCEPTION is specified, imperative-statement-2 is
executed if the ACCEPT operation finishes with a normal completion.
