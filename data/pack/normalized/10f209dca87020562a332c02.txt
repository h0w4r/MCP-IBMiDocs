Indicators Not Defined on the RPG IV Specifications

Not all indicators that can be used as conditioning indicators
in an RPG IV program are defined on the specification forms. External
indicators (U1 through U8) are defined by a CL command or by a previous RPG IV program.
Internal indicators (1P, LR, MR, and RT) are defined by the RPG IV program
cycle itself.
