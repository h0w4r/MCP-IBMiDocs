DECLARATIVES and END DECLARATIVES
DECLARATIVES and END DECLARATIVES are keywords that begin and end the declaratives part of the source program. In the Procedure Division, each of these words must begin in Area A and be followed immediately by a separator period; no other text may appear on the same line. After END DECLARATIVES, no text may appear before the following section header. (See Declaratives.)
