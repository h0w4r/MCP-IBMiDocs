COMMON Clause
The COMMON clause allows the program named by program-name to be called
by its siblings and by programs contained within the
siblings. The COMMON clause can be used only in nested programs.

Parent topic: PROGRAM-ID Paragraph

1 Sibling programs are those that are directly contained by the
same program.
