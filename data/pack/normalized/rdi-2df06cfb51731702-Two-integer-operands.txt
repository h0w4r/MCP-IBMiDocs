Two integer operands
If both operands of an arithmetic operator are integers
with zero scale, the operation is performed in binary, and the result
is a large integer unless either (or both) operand is a big integer,
in which case the result is a big integer. Any remainder of division
is lost. The result of an integer arithmetic operation (including unary
minus) must be within the range of large or big integers.
If either integer operand has nonzero scale, it is converted to a
decimal operand with the same precision and scale.
