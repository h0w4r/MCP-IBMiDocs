COLUMN_PRIVILEGES
The COLUMN_PRIVILEGES view contains one row for every privilege
granted on a column. Note that this catalog view cannot be used to
determine whether a user is authorized to a column because the privilege
to use a column could be acquired through a group user profile or
special authority (such as *ALLOBJ). Furthermore, the privilege to
use a column is also acquired through privileges granted on the table.
