The DDR Option
The DDR option or the SUBSTITUTE option does everything that the DD option
does. It also replaces the invalid COBOL characters @, #, $, and _ in a field
name (or alias name, if applicable) with the corresponding valid COBOL characters
A, N, D, and -. As well, it removes underscores from the end of a field name.
