Removing trace information from the system
To specify whether the trace information is removed
from the system or left on the system after the information is displayed,
use the Display Trace Data (DSPTRCDTA) command.
If you leave the trace information on the system, any other
traces are added to it. The information remains on the system (unless removed)
until the debug job ends or the ENDDBG command is submitted. You can also
use the Clear Trace Data (CLRTRCDTA) command to remove
trace information from the system.
