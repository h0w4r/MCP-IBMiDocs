AT END Phrases
The imperative-statement specified on the AT END phrase executes after
all records have been returned from file-name-1. No more RETURN statements
may be executed as part of the current output procedure.
If an at end condition does not occur during the execution of a RETURN
statement, then after the record is made available and after executing any
implicit move resulting from the presence of an INTO phrase, control is transferred
to the imperative statement specified by the NOT AT END phrase, otherwise
control is passed to
the end of the RETURN statement.
