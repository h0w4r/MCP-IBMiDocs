Turning on Return Codes during Record I/O

Exceptions are expensive to process.
If you use record I/O, you can minimize exceptions by using the
rtncode=y option on _Ropen(). Exceptions are not
generated for the following conditions:

"Record not found" (CPF5006)
"End-of-File" (CPF5001)

When these conditions occur, the num_bytes field of the
_RIOFB_T structure is updated and errno is set, but no
exceptions are generated. For the "Record not found"
condition, the num_bytes field is set to zero. For the
"End-of-File" condition, the num_bytes field is set to
EOF.
