NOCHGPOSSGN / CHGPOSSGN
NOCHGPOSSGN indicates that hexadecimal
F is used as the default positive sign for zoned and packed numeric data.
Hexadecimal F is the system default for the IBM® i operating system. NOCHGPOSSGN
is the default value.
CHGPOSSGN indicates that hexadecimal C is used as
the default positive sign for zoned and packed numeric data. This applies
to all results of the MOVE, ADD, SUBTRACT, MULTIPLY, DIVIDE, COMPUTE, and
INITIALIZE statements, as well as the results of the VALUE clause.
