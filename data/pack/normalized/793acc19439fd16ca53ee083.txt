ASIN
The ASIN function returns a numeric value in radians that approximates
the arcsine of the argument specified.
The function type is numeric.

Format

>>-FUNCTION ASIN--(--argument-1--)-----------------------------><

argument-1
Must be class numeric. The value of argument-1 must be greater than
or equal to -1 and less than or equal to +1.

The returned value is the approximation of the arcsine of argument-1, and
is greater than or equal to -Pi/2 and less than or equal to +Pi/2.
