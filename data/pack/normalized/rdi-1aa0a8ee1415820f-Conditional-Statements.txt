Conditional Statements
A conditional statement specifies that the truth
value of a condition is to be determined, and that the subsequent action of
the object program is dependent on this truth value. (See Conditional Expressions.)
Figure 1 lists COBOL statements that are conditional, or that
become conditional when a condition is included (for example: ON SIZE ERROR,
or ON OVERFLOW) and the statement is not terminated by its explicit scope
terminator.
