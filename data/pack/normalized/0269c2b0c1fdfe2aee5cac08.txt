EJECT Statement
The EJECT statement specifies that the next source statement is to be printed
at the top of the next page.

EJECT Statement - Format

>>-EJECT--+---+------------------------------------------------><
    '-.-'   

The EJECT statement must be the only statement on the line. It may be written
in either Area A or Area B, and may be terminated with a separator period.
The EJECT statement has no effect on the compilation of the source program
itself.
