ASCENDING/DESCENDING KEY Phrase Rules
When the ASCENDING/DESCENDING KEY phrase is specified, the following rules
apply:
Keys must be listed in decreasing order of significance.
You must arrange the data in the table in ASCENDING or DESCENDING sequence
according to the collating sequence in use.
A key can have DISPLAY, BINARY, PACKED-DECIMAL, or COMPUTATIONAL usage.

IBM ExtensionIBM Extension The KEY phrase can be specified in the OCCURS clause for a DBCS item.
A key can have COMPUTATIONAL-1, COMPUTATIONAL-2, COMPUTATIONAL-3,
COMPUTATIONAL-4, or COMPUTATIONAL-5 usage.
A key can have DISPLAY-1 usage.
A key can be an item of class date-time.
