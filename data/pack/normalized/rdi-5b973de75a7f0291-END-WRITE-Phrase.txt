END-WRITE Phrase
This explicit scope terminator serves to delimit the scope of the WRITE
statement. END-WRITE permits a conditional WRITE statement to be nested in
another conditional statement. END-WRITE may also be used with an imperative
WRITE statement.
For more information, see Delimited Scope Statements.
