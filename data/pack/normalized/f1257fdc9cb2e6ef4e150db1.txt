The Display Audit Journal Entries command to generate security journal
audit reports
The Display Audit Journal Entries (DSPAUDJRNE) command
allows you to generate security journal audit reports.
The reports generated by the Display Audit Journal Entries
(DSPAUDJRNE) command are based on the audit entry types and the
user profile that are specified on the command. You can limit reports to specific
time frames, and you can search detached journal receivers. You can direct
these reports to the active display or an output queue. RESTRICTIONS: You must have *ALLOBJ and *AUDIT special authorities to use
this command.
