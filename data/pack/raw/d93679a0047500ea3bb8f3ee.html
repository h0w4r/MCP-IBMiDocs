Indicator Area (Column 7)

Use the indicator area to specify:
  • The continuation of lines from the previous line onto the current line (see the information on continuation lines on page Continuation Lines)
  • The treatment of text as documentation or comments (see the information on comments on page Comment Lines)
  • Debugging lines (see the information on debugging lines on page Debugging Lines)
