Automatic Removal Of Watch Conditions

Watches are also removed in the following ways:

  • The CL End Debug (ENDDBG) command removes watches that are set in the local job or in a service job.
    Note:
    ENDDBG will be called automatically in abnormal situations to ensure that all affected watches are removed.
  • The initial program load (IPL) of your iSeries system removes all watch conditions system-wide.
