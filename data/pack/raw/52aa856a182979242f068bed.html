MONOPRC / NOMONOPRC

  • MONOPRC indicates monocasing of PROGRAM-ID and CALL/CANCEL target. MONOPRC is the default value.
  • NOMONOPRC indicates no monocasing of PROGRAM-ID and CALL/CANCEL target. This option is most useful when dealing with external calls to case sensitive procedure names (for example: C functions).
