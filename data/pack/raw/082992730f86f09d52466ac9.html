CHGDEVTAP UNLOAD change now allowed while device is varied on

Last Updated: 2025-10-07

With IBM® i 7.3 PTF MF70242, the Change Device Desc (Tape) command (CHGDEVTAP) will now allow the UNLOAD (Unload device at vary off) parameter to be modified while the device is varied on.
