Display Trademarks (DSPTM)

Last Updated: 2025-04-08
Where allowed to run: All environments (*ALL)
Threadsafe: No 		Parameters

The Display Trademarks (DSPTM) command displays a list of trademarks that appear in the names of licensed products.

There are no parameters for this command.
