IBM i job scheduler and scheduled job commands

Comandos de IBM i para trabajar con job schedule entries, planificar trabajos, cambiar/remover entradas planificadas y revisar jobs sometidos o scheduler avanzado.

Intenciones cubiertas

  • How do I inspect scheduled jobs in IBM i?
  • Which commands manage job schedule entries?
  • How can I verify if one batch job runs before or after another?
  • How do I submit or review scheduled/submitted jobs?

Comandos principales

  • ADDJOBSCDE: Add Job Schedule Entry.
  • CHGJOBSCDE: Change Job Schedule Entry.
  • RMVJOBSCDE: Remove Job Schedule Entry.
  • WRKJOBSCDE: Work with Job Schedule Entries.
  • SBMJOB: Submit Job.
  • WRKSBMJOB: Work with Submitted Jobs.
  • DSPJOB: Display Job.
  • WRKJOB: Work with Job.
  • DSPJOBLOG: Display Job Log.
  • DSPHSTJS: Display History using Advanced Job Scheduler.
  • DSPJOBJS: Display Job using Advanced Job Scheduler.
  • STRJS: Start Advanced Job Scheduler.
  • ENDJS: End Advanced Job Scheduler.
  • HLDJOB: Hold Job.
  • RLSJOB: Release Job.
  • CHGJOB: Change Job.
