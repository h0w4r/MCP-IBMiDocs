Changes to CL commands sending messages to *EXT

Last Updated: 2025-10-07

IBM i CL commands document Escape messages that occur, but other messages issued are not documented.

Diagnostic messages may accompany these messages to provide additional information. Messages reflecting run-time activities or status are also occasionally issued.

These information, status, and diagnostic messages are not documented and can be issued to the joblog, QSYSOPR, QHST, and *EXT. The type and location of these messages occasionally change to support new function at a release boundary or via PTF.

Customer programs written to retrieve the info, status, or diagnostic messages issued by CL commands may be required to be modified when the CL commands are changed. Unless the changes are related to Escape messages that are normally documented for the CL command, the changes are not always documented specifically by an update to the Memo to Users (MTU) or in a PTF cover letter.
