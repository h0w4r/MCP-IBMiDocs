FILE-CONTROL Paragraph - Format 1 - Sequential

                          '-OPTIONAL-'              

                   .-----------------------.       
                   V                       | (1)   
>--ASSIGN--+----+----+-assignment-name-1-+-+-------------------->
           '-TO-'    '-literal-1---------'         

>--+---------------------------------+-------------------------->
   |         (2)                     |

                           +-AREA--+

     '-ORGANIZATION--+----+-'                 
                     '-IS-'                   

>--+-----------------------------------------------------+------>
   |         (2)                                         |

>--+--------------------------------------+--------------------->
