Built-in functions for CL

Control language (CL) provides several built-in functions. Built-in functions are used in arithmetic, relational, or logical expressions.

CL provides the following built-in functions:
  • %ADDRESS
  • %BINARY
  • %OFFSET
  • %SUBSTRING
  • %SWITCH
