Positions 29, 37, and 45 (Code Part)

Entry
Explanation
C
Entire character
Z
Zone portion of character
D
Digit portion of character.

This entry specifies what part of the character in the record identification code is to be tested.

  • Character (C)
  • Zone (Z)
  • Digit (D)
