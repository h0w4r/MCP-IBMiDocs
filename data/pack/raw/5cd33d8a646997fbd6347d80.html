OCCURS Clause

The Data Division clauses that are used for table handling are the OCCURS clause and USAGE IS INDEX clause (For the USAGE IS INDEX description, see USAGE Clause.) Format 1 of the OCCURS clause handles fixed-length tables. Format 2 of the OCCURS clause handles variable-length tables.

The OCCURS Clause has two formats:
  • Format 1 - Fixed Length Tables
  • Format 2 - Variable Length Tables
